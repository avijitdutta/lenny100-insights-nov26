# The Lenny 100, mapped

A one-page landscape of the 99 companies on the [Lenny 100](https://www.lennysjobs.com/lenny100) list from Lenny’s Jobs, as of 13 November 2026.

The page is organized in six sections:

1. **The map**: the companies as a supply chain of twelve layers in five groups, with a chart of size against hiring speed.
2. **How solid the numbers are**: what market prices, last-round prices and headcount growth can and cannot tell you.
3. **Value and growth**: where the value sits, where the hiring is, and where the two rankings diverge.
4. **How they make money**: revenue models, what to measure for each, and who pays.
5. **Five findings**: value concentration, growth by size, who beats the trend, category races, and which companies get talked about.
6. **All companies**: a sortable, filterable table with links to each company’s website.

## Viewing it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or visit the published GitHub Pages site. Everything is in that one file, with the data embedded. It loads D3 v7.9.0 from cdnjs and the IBM Plex and Source Serif fonts from Google Fonts, so it needs an internet connection.

## Sources and method

- Company names, descriptions, valuations, headcount growth and listing status come from the Lenny 100 list. They have not been independently verified.
- Layers, groups, revenue models, main buyers, category races and the open-source flag are editorial classifications, not reported data.
- Website links are unverified. Where no official site was identified, the link opens a web search.
- Valuations combine public market capitalizations with private last-round prices, which are different kinds of figure.
