The map: twelve layers in five groups

Read the list as a supply chain. Chips and power feed the GPU clouds, the clouds feed the model labs, and the labs feed the developer tools and applications built on top. A newer wave carries the same models into robots, vehicles, ships and weapons, while the money rails run beneath all of it.

02

How solid are the numbers?

Market prices

Public companies are repriced every trading day by thousands of buyers and sellers. These are the firmest figures here, though they still carry the market’s mood.

Last-round prices

A private valuation is a single negotiated price on a single day, often with terms that flatter the headline. It records what the most optimistic investor paid, not what the company would fetch in a sale.

Headcount growth

Hiring is visible and hard to fake, which makes it the best common signal of momentum on the list. But it tracks ambition and funding, not revenue: a well-funded lab can triple its staff on capital alone.

For people choosing where to work

The fastest hiring is at smaller AI-built companies, where each hire shapes the product. Established names offer scale and stability rather than the steepest learning curve.

For buyers

Vendors increasingly charge for results (a resolved ticket, a drafted contract, a shipped app) rather than for seats. That shifts risk to the vendor and puts the focus on outcomes.

For investors

Value is concentrated in a handful of compute owners and labs. Much of the list is a wager that value will spread to the companies applying models, and prices do not yet reflect it.
